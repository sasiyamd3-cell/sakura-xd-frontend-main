About the bot

What Miyora MD does

Auto status view

Quietly views your contacts' status updates the moment they're posted, so you never miss one.

Auto status react

Reacts to statuses with a set of emojis you choose, so you're never left on "seen" only.

View-once unlock

Automatically reveals view-once photos and videos sent to you, saved right back into the chat.

Flexible reply modes

Switch between public, private, inbox-only, or groups-only — your bot, your rules.

Stays online

Auto-reconnects if your session drops, with a duplicate-session guard so it never fights itself across hosts.

Private by number

Each WhatsApp number gets its own settings, locked behind a password only you receive.

Newsletter tools

Follows and reacts to channels automatically, keeping your presence active across WhatsApp.

Modular commands

Every command lives in its own file, so new features drop in without touching the core.
